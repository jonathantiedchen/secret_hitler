Behavior Notes

  • Speed Mode: "Realtime" shows delay slider; "Fast" hides it and runs at max speed
  • Toggle switches: Dark = on, light = off
  • Show AI Thoughts: Default ON - this is a key feature for spectating
  • Reveal Roles: Default OFF - preserves suspense
  • Settings persist: Saved to localStorage for next session
  • Sensible defaults: New users can just click "Review & Start"
