Visual Elements

  • Model Icons: Each AI model gets a unique emoji/icon and color (Llama=🦙 purple, GPT=🤖 green, Claude=🎭 pink, Mistral=🔷 orange, Gemini=💎 cyan)
  • Table Layout: Characters arranged around a virtual table - feels like watching a game
  • Thought Bubbles: Active player shows their current thinking above their avatar
  • Role Badges: President 👑, Chancellor 🎩, Dead 💀 clearly visible
  • Glowing Active: Current player's avatar pulses/glows to show it's their turn
  • Dark Theme: Dramatic atmosphere matching the game's tone
